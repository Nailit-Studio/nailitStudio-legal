Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026  ·  England & Wales

1. Who we are

NailitStudio is an independent software product built for job seekers, developed and operated by NailitStudio (contact: Nailit-Studio@outlook.com). We are committed to full compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What data we collect

We collect no personal data on our servers. All data you enter into NailitStudio is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. We do not transmit, store, or process your email content on any server we operate. No account registration is required to use the app.

3. Local AI classification

AI-powered email classification runs using a model embedded in the app, powered by Apple's Natural Language framework. No email content, metadata, or personal information leaves your device for classification purposes. All processing happens entirely on-device.

4. Advertising (free tier)

The free tier of NailitStudio displays contextual advertising. We do not use third-party tracking pixels, behavioural profiling, or sell your data to advertisers. Pro subscribers are shown no advertising whatsoever.

5. Third-party email connections (Gmail & Outlook)

If you choose to connect a Gmail or Outlook account via OAuth, we request read-only access scoped to job-related emails only. We never send emails on your behalf.

OAuth tokens (the credentials used to access your email) are stored securely in your device's local Keychain — they are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. You may revoke access at any time via your Google or Microsoft account security settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of deletion. To erase all app data, go to Settings → Clear all data within the app, or delete the app from your device entirely. We hold no server-side copy of your data and therefore cannot delete it on your behalf.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing of your personal data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object to processing

Because we hold no server-side data, these rights are exercised by clearing your local app storage as described in Section 6. If you have any questions about your rights, contact us at Nailit-Studio@outlook.com.

8. Third-party services

Use of Gmail is subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Use of Outlook is subject to Microsoft's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. NailitStudio is not responsible for the data practices of these third-party services.

9. Children

NailitStudio is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notification. Continued use of NailitStudio after notification of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised.
