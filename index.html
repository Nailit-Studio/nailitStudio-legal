NailitStudio

NailitStudio is a privacy-first job application manager for iOS. It connects to your Gmail or Outlook account via read-only OAuth access and uses on-device AI to automatically classify and organise your job-related emails. All email data is processed and stored locally on your device — nothing is sent to our servers.

NailitStudio helps job seekers track every application, interview, and offer in one place, without sharing your email data with anyone.
